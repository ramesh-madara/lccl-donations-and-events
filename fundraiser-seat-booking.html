TICKET PURCHASE AND COLLECTION INSTRUCTIONS

Complete Payment Details:

Please fill out all the required fields in the payment form accurately.

Make sure to enter your correct First Name, Last Name, Mobile Number, and Email Address.

Transaction Confirmation:

After successful payment, you will receive a transaction confirmation email and SMS.

Keep Your Transaction ID Handy:

Please make a note of the unique Transaction ID provided in your confirmation email and SMS. This ID is essential for ticket collection.

Ticket Collection on Event Day:

Tickets can be collected at the designated ticket counter on the day of the charity fundraising event. Please note that ticket collection is available only one hour before the event starts.

Verification Process:

To collect your tickets, you will be required to verify your transaction.

Email Verification: Show a printed or digital copy of the transaction confirmation email at the ticket counter.

SMS Verification: You can also show the SMS confirmation message on your mobile device.

Collecting Tickets for Multiple Seats:

If you have purchased tickets for more than one person, please ensure that you collect all the tickets at the designated ticket counter.

It is your responsibility to share the tickets with everyone in your group. Each individual will need their ticket for entry to the event.

Lost Confirmation Email or SMS:

In case you lose the email or SMS, you may be asked to provide the Mobile Number used for the transaction at the ticket counter for verification.

Contact Information:

If you encounter any issues or have questions regarding ticket collection, please contact our customer support team at +94 71 482 2283.

Important Reminder:

Please keep your confirmation email and SMS in a safe and easily accessible place until the event day. They are your proof of purchase and will be required for ticket collection.
